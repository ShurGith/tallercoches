# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
- **Assumption, unconfirmed:** a workshop owner or manager is the primary user; reception and workshop staff also use the system according to their responsibilities.

## Product Purpose
Organize a vehicle workshop's customer records, estimates, appointments, parts inventory, staff, and working hours in one application.

## Positioning
No distinct market position or product claim has been established.

## Operating Context
- **Assumption, unconfirmed:** the team uses the application during day-to-day workshop administration to find customer and vehicle information, coordinate work, and monitor operational tasks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Planned capabilities include customer records and history, estimates, a calendar and reminders, inventory movements and low-stock alerts, employee records, schedules, roles, and permissions.
- The frontend is React with Tailwind CSS; the API is Node.js/Express and the database is PostgreSQL on Supabase.
- Deployment approach is undecided; Docker may be considered later.
- Synthetic data is for demonstration and testing only, not evidence about real customers or business performance.

## Brand Commitments
- The product name is TallerCoches.
- No visual identity, logo, or other brand rules have been established.

## Evidence on Hand
- No real customer, commercial, performance, or service claims have been supplied. Any panel data created for this work must be visibly synthetic.

## Product Principles
- Keep customer, vehicle, and workshop-operational information easy to find.
- Make task status and upcoming work legible to the team.
- Keep synthetic examples distinguishable from real records.
- Preserve access boundaries for staff roles as the application grows.
